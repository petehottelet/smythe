# Jobs scale publication plan

Publish the outcome of the [frozen recovery workload](../benchmarks/jobs_scale_benchmark.md),
including an incomplete or failed outcome. The first full campaign and any
later campaign keep separate directories and records. No comparative speed or
model-quality claim follows from this local fixture run.

## Evidence package

Keep the raw result JSON unchanged. Archive the complete campaign directory,
including worker logs, configuration, provenance, provider-entry records,
SQLite files, and accepted PNGs. An external inventory records each member's
size and SHA-256, plus the archive and raw-result hashes. Verify archive members
against the original bytes. Inspect SQLite using an extracted copy; do not
open the archived evidence in a mode that can change its journal files.

An independent review must reconcile operation and call identities, accepted
pointers, artifacts, attempt lineage, the real kill and lease-expiry records,
and all zero-cost balances before promoting completion assertions. Keep the
runtime commit, source hashes, host, and one-campaign scope beside the result.

## Monochrome figure

If the full campaign passes review, add a figure generated by
`python benchmarks/render_readme_charts.py` from the committed result and its
review record. Use the existing black-and-white [house style](../docs/style.md):
Georgia title, Trajan headline numerals, compact sans-serif labels, square
rules, and no gray or gradients.

Show three observed stages: after the kill, after safe resume, and after
explicit rerolls. Draw accepted counts against the same zero-to-operation-count
axis. Label pending and interrupted/unknown counts directly, including any
small counts that a full-width bar cannot show legibly. The interrupted calls
at the kill point are still dispatched; label them unknown only after recovery.

Derive headline values from the record: final accepted artifacts, explicit
rerolls, previously accepted work reissued, and new calls on completed resume.
Print the sample size (one local campaign), fixture contract (identical 1×1
PNGs), source record, and content hash on the figure. Do not depict identical
fixture files as distinct generated glyphs or infer production-image capacity.

## Documentation

Update the benchmark guide/index, README recovery discussion, Jobs guide,
roadmap, changelog, examples index, and completion tracker. Preserve the
existing matched framework recovery comparison. Keep the scale observation
separate from that comparative evidence and from glyph-generation timing.
